# Java Study Buddy 📚

**Java Study Buddy** is a student-focused study assistant designed to make learning, revision, and self-assessment easier. It helps students manage their last minute hasel and turn their study materials into structured notes, practice questions, and revision resources in one place.

## ✨ Features

### Must-Have Features

- **Document Upload and Reading:** Upload study documents and extract their text for processing.
- **Smart Notes and Summarization:** Convert lengthy study materials into concise revision notes.
- **Quiz and MCQ Generation:** Practice questions based on study material and evaluate performance.
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor quiz scores, completed sessions, and learning progress.
- **Flashcards and Quick Revision:** Review important concepts using question-and-answer flashcards.

### Should-Have Features

- **Viva Preparation Mode:** Prepare for oral examinations using document-based questions and sample answers.
- **Focus Study Mode:** Study in focused sessions using a timer and scheduled breaks.
- **Personalized Study Recommendations:** Identify topics that need more revision based on performance.
- **Ask Questions About Your Document:** Ask questions about uploaded material and receive relevant answers.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Simplify the revision of lengthy study materials.
- Help students assess their understanding of concepts.
- Encourage active recall through quizzes and flashcards.
- Track learning progress over time.
- Provide a structured and focused study experience.
